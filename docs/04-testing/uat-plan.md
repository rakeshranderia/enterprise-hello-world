# UAT Plan

## Purpose

Confirm that the service satisfies the agreed business requirement.

## UAT Participants

For this demonstration, the repository owner may act as:

- business owner;
- UAT tester.

In a real organisation these roles may be separated.

## UAT Scenario

### UAT-001

**Given** the service is available  
**When** I open the root URL  
**Then** I see exactly:

```text
Hello World
```

## Evidence

Record:

- date;
- version/commit;
- result;
- screenshot if useful;
- defect issue if failed.

## Exit Criteria

- UAT-001 passes;
- any release-blocking defect is resolved;
- business acceptance recorded.
